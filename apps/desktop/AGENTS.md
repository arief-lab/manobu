# apps/desktop

## Purpose

- React 19 single-page app for manobu, file-routed with TanStack Router. Runs in two modes:
  - Vite dev server on port `3001` (web-only).
  - Tauri desktop shell (Tauri v2 sources at `src-tauri/`).

## Ownership

- Project contributors.

TASK-147 added the optional `level` to `IntentResult`: each layer already computed one and dropped it, so the composer's exam branch reads it instead of re-parsing the text. - `paper-notice.ts` — TASK-148 AC#6, extended by TASK-173.4: `paperBuildNotice(receipt)` is the ONE mapping from an assembly receipt to the `{ text, tone }` the `/ai` composer prints (`Pick`ed to the five fields it reads: `contentExhausted`, `ready`, `totalSlots`, `unfilledBlocks`, `validatedSlots`, so it is a consumer of the contract, not of the route's local state). The rule it exists to enforce: a paper the level could not fill is never announced as one that was. `ready` → the size (`Paper ready: 30 items.`, TASK-156's "describe a paper with the number the host gave it") at tone `success`; `!ready && unfilledBlocks.length > 0` → both numbers and EACH missing 大問 named through `examSectionLabel` (`Vocabulary 大問 2 (表記)`), plus the practice-paper alternative, at tone `warning`; `!ready && contentExhausted` → both numbers plus the reason the learner can act on (`This level has no new questions left.`) at tone `warning`; `!ready` for any other reason → the build failure at tone `error`, because a validator rejection or a missing corpus is not a statement about how much of the level the learner has seen. The order of the two warning arms is the contract: a real paper short by whole 大問 has a hole in the PLAN, and telling that learner they ran the level dry blames their history for something no amount of new content fixes. "Try again?" is never the sentence for a structural hole, because the next attempt is the same hole. The tone is what stops the composer notice painting a success in the error red (see `packages/chat-session/AGENTS.md` on `sendNotice`). Tests: `test/paper-notice.test.ts` (6 cases: one per state, the missing-大問 case including its precedence over exhaustion, a "never says ready when the receipt says not", and a tone-per-state).
