# Reading supply decisions: what the coverage floor measures, what the 白書 can print, and which source is licensed

Decisions taken 2026-10-08 on the user instruction to find the corpus behind the five remaining 読解 holes. **The evidence lives in doc-26 §4; this doc is the decision record, so a worker can act without the conversation that produced it.** Work is split into TASK-173.9.12, TASK-173.9.13, TASK-173.9.14 and TASK-173.9.15.

## 1. The measurement every decision rests on

The store's own difficulty instrument, `coverageOf` (`packages/japanese/scripts/reading-chuubun-corpus.ts:377`), was pointed at the official 2018 読解 sample papers that doc-26 §1 already pins (`https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sample2018/pdf/N1R.pdf`, `.../N3R.pdf`), extracted with `pdftotext -layout`. **No official text was vendored; this is a calibration, not a source.**

| what was scored | measured | the floor it is judged by | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| five official N1 読解 texts (1052-2716 runes) | 86.0% to 89.1% | `COVERAGE_FLOOR.N1 = 0.9` (`reading-chuubun-corpus.ts:104`) | **every one fails the bar we set** |
| official N3 中文 (382 runes) and N3 長文 (658 runes) | 87.7% and 88.9% | `COVERAGE_FLOOR.N3 = 0.8` | clears with room |
| official N1 情報素材 (the four-library 案内, 1105 runes) | 89.1% | 0.9 | clears; the 素材 genre is not coverage-hostile |
| 白書 windows, 900-1200 runes, 2-5 consecutive paragraphs | 1826 windows, ceiling 88.9%, 165 at ≥85%, **0 at ≥90%** | 0.9 | unreachable by construction |
| 白書 windows, 500-700 runes, same method | 1747 windows, ceiling 79.9%, **0 at ≥80%**, p90 73.9% | 0.8 | 8 points harder than real N3 text |

Caveats stated rather than hidden: the passage extraction is line-based and heuristic, the 2018 set's 大問 markers are partly garbled (doc-26 §1 already warns), and the instrument charges every unmatched non-kana character, so digits and the sample PDF's mangled number encoding cost coverage. Treat the official figures as ±2 points. They do not need more precision than that to make the point, because the entire official N1 sample sits below 0.9.

## 2. D-1. The N1 長文 hole is an instrument error, not a shortage

A 0.9 floor at N1 rejects authentic N1 exam prose, so it was never satisfiable at 長文 length by any source. **Decision: 長文 gets its own floor, 0.85, declared per block, with this measurement cited as its derivation in the module that declares it.** This is NOT a global `COVERAGE_FLOOR` cut: `COVERAGE_FLOOR.N1 = 0.9` keeps governing 中文, 短文, 主張理解 and 統合理解, whose shipped stores are screened by it today and stay at that claim.

What 0.85 buys, in the corpus already in the tree: 165 admissible windows in the 900-1200 band against the 4 that N1 大問10 prints (`packages/item-engine/src/paper.ts:513`).

The cost is stated, not buried: the shipped N1 stores mediate 90.0% (`n1.json`), 90.2% (`n1-chuubun.json`) and 91.6% (`n1-chuushou.json`), which is EASIER than anything the real exam prints. Lowering the 長文 floor does not change those rows, and no future work may present those stores as exam-difficulty without re-measuring them against the official distribution.

## 3. D-2. A 長文 passage may be a multi-paragraph document

The corpus is indexed per `<p>` paragraph and every shipped reading store spends ONE paragraph per 小問, which is right for 短文 and tolerable for 中文. A real 長文 is several paragraphs of one section. **Decision: the 長文 generator assembles 2 to 5 CONSECUTIVE prose paragraphs from one pinned page** (each at least 150 runes so statistic-table fragments cannot pad a document, none already spent by another store), and `sourceIds` names every paragraph so the attribution stays exact and the TASK-148 no-re-serve rule holds. This alone moved the N1 band from 1 usable single paragraph to 1826 windows, which is why the single-paragraph rule was worth naming as an assumption rather than inheriting it.

## 4. D-3. The N3 長文 hole is a genre failure and the floor stays

Official N3 text clears 0.8 at 87.7-88.9%, so the floor is correct; the 白書's long prose tops out at 79.9% because its register is administrative (生物多様性国家戦略, イベント・アトリビューション, スーパーコンピュータ). **Decision: do not lower the N3 floor, because that would admit material 8 points harder than the exam while claiming to prepare for it.** N3 大問6 (`paper.ts:565`) is a sourcing question, measured by TASK-173.9.14, and the refusal is explicit: fewer than 4 clearing documents leaves the 大問 named in the unfilled receipt, and 中文-length material never gets printed under a 長文 heading (doc-26 already refuses the 380-rune band shortcut for the same reason).

**Measured (TASK-173.9.14, 2026-10-08): AC#4 holds, the 大問 stays named, and the reason for it moves.** `bun run probe:n3-choubun` (`packages/japanese/scripts/probe-n3-choubun-source.ts`) ran three non-環境省 arms through the shipped instrument and the shipped `buildChoubunBatch` (only its floor overridden), at the official 大問6 band 500-700 runes: a seeded unbiased draw of 150 ja.wikipedia articles (CC BY-SA 4.0), the 情報通信白書 令和6年版 (verified PDL1.0) and the 警察白書 令和6年版 (licence unverified). The full table, method and licence accounting are in doc-26 §4. **The verdict: at `CHOUBUN_COVERAGE_FLOOR` = 0.85 the entire three-arm supply is 1 clearing window and 1 disjoint buildable row, best 85.6%, so N3 大問6 cannot print its 4 小問, no floor is lowered, no 中文-length text goes under a 長文 heading, and nothing ships.** Two consequences change what a future worker should try, and both are why this paragraph exists rather than a one-line "refused".

**(1) The genre finding generalises, so a fourth source will measure the same.** A random encyclopedia article, a second ministry's 白書 and the police 白書 all top out in the same 82-86% band at 550-character length, so the 79.9% 環境白書 ceiling was never a register artefact of one corpus: unedited authentic Japanese carries 14-18% out-of-list runes at 長文 length. Read against §1, that means the official paper's 88.9% is authored simplicity rather than the language's norm, and the same logic that made D-1 a 長文-specific floor at N1 applies here in reverse: at N3 the bar is not too high for the exam, it is higher than unedited prose can be.

**(2) At the level's own `COVERAGE_FLOOR.N3` = 0.8 bar there IS supply: 8 mutually disjoint buildable rows from 8 distinct pages, 6 of them under a licence this repo can ship today.** So N3 大問6 stopped being a sourcing question the moment the source was found, and what is left is a policy question with §2's guard on it: a 長文-specific N3 floor may only be declared against its own official-text measurement, never to launder a shortage. The one supply-side route the data supports is scale, and it is named rather than started: ~1 buildable row per 150 articles means ~600 articles for 4 rows at 0.85, ~50 minutes of polite sequential fetching at 120 ms per page, reproducible from `seed=173914`, and it would put CC BY-SA share-alike on the derived passage files. Extractability is not the obstacle: 84-94% of fetched ministry pages carry a ≥150-rune prose paragraph.

## 5. D-4. 情報検索 ships from real PDL1.0 情報素材, with three obligations

The obstacle at N2 大問14 / N1 大問13 was never the item shape; `info-search` exists (`paper.ts:330`) and the 素材 genre measures easy (89.1%). **Decision: build the store from real government 情報素材 rather than authoring one, because the genre is the ねらい.** PDL1.0 settles the legal question in our favour twice over: 「数値データ、簡単な表・グラフ等は著作権による保護の対象ではありません」, so a fee or schedule table is an unprotected fact set, and redistribution plus modification plus shipping in a repo is allowed with attribution. Three obligations attach to every row, and TASK-173.9.13 makes them acceptance criteria: the 出典 credit with the exact page URL, a statement that THIS repo edited or processed the table (編集・加工等を行ったこと and its actor) so it is never presented as unprocessed government material, and an 取得日 stamp, because 令和8年 保険料 figures go stale silently.

**Shipped (TASK-173.9.13, 2026-10-08), with one deviation to record.** `packages/japanese/data/reading/{n2,n1}-jouhou.json` each carry 6 rows over 3 素材 (国税庁 印紙税額 and 源泉徴収税額 tables, a 文部科学省 就学支援金 Q&A at N2; 厚生労働省 地域別最低賃金 全国一覧 and 国民年金 前納額 notices, 出入国在留管理庁 在留資格 一覧表 at N1), 685-774 runes, each printed cell proven against the vendored snapshot and each row carrying the three obligations as FIELDS, not comments: `sourceCredit` with the page URL, `editedCredit` stating the 編集・加工 and naming manobu as the actor with 取得日 inside it, and `acquiredAt`. The deviation is the difficulty of the decision, not its direction: the coverage floor this store deliberately does NOT have. The 素材 genre measures 89.1% under the instrument (§1), so a floor would screen out the tables it exists to certify, and the screens that replaced it are decidability screens - the answer printed in the `key` line the stem names, no distractor in that line, no distractor readable from a DIFFERENT shipped 素材 (`packages/japanese/scripts/reading-jouhou-corpus.ts`). A second deviation is ours rather than upstream: the option ORDER is authored here, and authored to spread the key across all four positions per level, which `check:reading-answers` now audits for these two files. The blind judge round ran BEFORE that spread and passed all 12 rows with zero rejections; the verdicts are frozen in `{level}-jouhou-rejected.json` and the record discloses the ordering, because the option SETS are byte-identical and no verdict moves.

Fallback, authorised but not chosen: hand-authored 素材 labelled `composed` over real (uncopyrighted) fee and schedule figures. Not authorised: re-stamping the N5 notices upward, which `packages/japanese/data/LICENSE.md` §11 already refuses and which the widening would not launder.

## 6. D-5. N4 中文 composes, because nothing else can print it

The 白書 yields 0 clearing paragraphs at N4 even at a 0.7 floor (doc-26 §5), and the licensed upstream has no N4 中長文 papers at all. **Decision: compose over in-level licensed prose, following TASK-173.9.5, TASK-173.9.6 and TASK-173.9.10**: deterministic generator, screens shared between generator and CI gate, both blind-judge families run offline and their verdicts frozen as data, CI never runs a model.

**Shipped (TASK-173.9.15, 2026-10-08) as the SECOND construction inside the same 中文 pipeline.** `packages/japanese/data/reading/n4-chuubun.json` carries 12 rows labelled `construction: "stitched-in-level"`, each a 402-415 rune document assembled from 25-36 licensed in-level sentences (`data/sentences/sentences.jsonl`, Tatoeba CC BY 2.0 FR over Tanaka's PD corpus) that all print one noun, with one OTHER in-level noun blanked. Two tables decide the split and both halves of the pipeline read them: `CHUUBUN_SOURCE` (N1/N2/N3 → `hakusho`, N4 → `sentences`) picks the corpus per level, and `PASSAGE_BAND` gives a 白書 row 200-1200 runes and a stitch 400-600 (the official N4 中文 ねらい is 450字程度). A row may not choose its own proof: `construction-not-for-${level}` refuses an N4 store of 白書 rows and an N2 store of stitches, because each would claim the easier corpus.

The supply chain is measured, not assumed (`bun run measure:reading-supply`): 9,171 in-level sentences → 522 candidate topic nouns → 97 topics holding ≥30 sentences (`STITCH_MIN_TOPIC_SENTENCES`) → **56** rows the screens emit, i.e. 14 papers of 4 小問 (`packages/item-engine/src/paper.ts:589`). The store ships **12**, because 12 is what the blind judge covered: depth here is a judging budget, not a corpus limit, and a refill means judging more rows, never loosening a screen. `--count 12` reproduces the file byte for byte; `--count 13` appends an unjudged row.

Two obligations the decision carries, both proved: the floor is RE-MEASURED at N4 rather than inherited (`COVERAGE_FLOOR.N4 = 0.8`; of the 95 band documents the supply can stitch, all 95 score below 1.0 and the topic 年 tops out at 0.771, so the screen is a real refusal, and the 白書 comparison is 0 of 2035 in-band paragraphs clearing even 0.7), and every 白書 screen runs unchanged on the assembled text (restore-to-source byte equality over the concatenation of the cited sentences, the answer occurring once, in-level nouns, the distractor pool, the uniquely filled collocation frame, the stem-leak skip). What a stitch is NOT is stated in `packages/japanese/data/LICENSE.md` §19: a topic-coherent collage is not a narrative, the deciding evidence for these rows is the case frame of the ONE blanked sentence, and the rows are `composed`, never `licensed`.

The judge record discloses its own weakness: both passes are the same model family (`qoder/general-purpose`) under two deliberately different procedures, LESS independent than the two families behind §13's stores. Round A over all 12 rows: 11 PASS, one AMBIGUOUS (`n4c-77613`, where the two readers keyed 簡単 and 夜 differently, so the passage does not decide it and the row is burned); round B judged the replacement (`n4c-77922`, 先生) PASS under both procedures.

Filling N4 also cost the mutation gate a seam, and the cost is the lesson worth recording: `medium-reading-store-read-wrong-level` (`scripts/mutation-gate.ts:1190`, mutating `mediumReadingStoreFor(level)` at `apps/server/src/services/exam-service.ts:1465` to always read N2) **SURVIVED** the first run after the store shipped, because the property it pinned was proved by an ABSENCE. Until TASK-173.9.15 the test that caught it was N4 having no 中文 store at all, and the moment every level N5-N1 ships a file, an always-reads-N2 picker still returns a well-formed row stamped with the requested level, so nothing distinguished it. The seam was kept and the coverage strengthened rather than the mutation weakened (`test/exam-medium-reading.test.ts`, "reads the store of the level it was asked for, at every shipped level"): for each level the served `anchorId` must be a row of THAT level's own file, which is the only fact that survives a fully-populated corpus. Row identity, not emptiness, is the durable proof.

## 7. D-6. Flat and banded papers are not part of any of this

`long-reading` carries weight 20 in the N1 and N3 flat mixes and is served `LONG_READING_PASSAGE` (`apps/server/src/services/exam-service.ts:629`). Wiring real mode does not touch that, per the user's standing decision. **Any change to flat weights is out of scope for all four tasks**, and the canned passage stays reachable for `shortened` and `banded` papers so no flat-mode test moves.

## 10. D-7. N3 大問6 closes by authoring, because the floor was never measuring what the 大問 needs

This decision reverses the route, not the standard. Read with §4, which is the measurement that forced it.

**What the deterministic rule was actually for.** Everything the corpus pipeline proves is a provenance claim: restore-to-source byte equality, pinned sha256 snapshots, licence obligations carried as row FIELDS (§5), option-position spread audited by `check:reading-answers`, and a store file that regenerates identically from committed bytes. Those properties are worth keeping and none of them come from an LLM. What they never bought is a good item.

**Where the rule was wrong.** The coverage floor is an invented difficulty proxy. §1 measured official N3 長文 text at 87.7-88.9% under it and §4 measured unedited authentic Japanese at 82-86% across three independent arms, which means the floor does not screen for "exam-like"; it screens for "vocabulary-simple", a property a source search can decide and a reader cannot. A 長文 小問's actual quality is that the passage carries one argument across its paragraphs, that its 4 小問 hit distinct ねらい, and that each distractor fails for a specific recoverable reason. No coverage percentage sees any of that. So the pipeline was spending its one measurable knob to reject authentic text, and the 173.9.x stores that did ship inherit the same limit: a blanked slot in a 白書 paragraph trains slot recovery under a 中文 or 短文 heading.

**Decision: for N3 大問6 the store is authored, and determinism moves to where it is strong.** The agent writes 8 passages and their questions from scratch at the official band (550字程度, `paper.ts:565` prints 4 小問); a human reviews every row before anything ships, which is the judgment the floor was standing in for. The mechanical part stays deterministic and stays in CI: the existing decidability screens (answer uniqueness, distractor pool, stem-leak skip, frame solvability) run on the authored text because they encode "this item can be answered from its own passage", a property authoring does not exempt; the option order is still generated and still audited for key spread; the committed `n3-choubun.json` is frozen data that regenerates byte-for-byte. Each row carries `construction: "authored"` so the store never claims a provenance it does not have, the same discipline that makes a stitch `composed` rather than `licensed` (§6, LICENSE.md §19).

**Scope of the reversal, stated narrowly.** This supersedes §2's floor machinery for NEW stores only. Nothing shipped moves: the N1 白書 長文 rows, the 中文/短文 stores, and every floor constant stay exactly as they are, and `probe:n3-choubun` remains the committed record of what the deterministic route measured at this block. The floor is not lowered to make N3 pass; it is removed as the gate for a store that does not source its prose. Vocabulary control survives as a style guide for the authoring (cumulative N5-N3 readings, the same index `coverageOf` uses), not as a rejection rule.

**Depth: 8 rows, matching the block it joins.** `n1-choubun.json` ships 8 rows against a 4-小問 大問, i.e. two papers without re-serving (TASK-148), and every shipped reading store runs 2-3 papers deep. 8 authored rows put N3 at the same depth as its N1 sibling. More rows means more reviewed rows, never fewer screens, exactly as §6 states for the N4 stitch.

**What closing this does and does not mean.** Verified from source before this section was written: counting every reading store on disk against the blueprint demand (`packages/item-engine/src/paper.ts:501` `PAPER_BLOCKS`, reading entries N1 `:511-516`, N2 `:537-541`, N3 `:563-566`, N4 `:588-590`, N5 `:610-612`), `n3-choubun.json` is the ONLY missing reading store at any level, and `choubunStoreFor` already loops N5..N1 (`apps/server/src/services/exam-corpus.ts:580-591`) with the real-mode picker returning null on an empty store (`apps/server/src/services/exam-service.ts:1556`) and the item-type map already routing `long-reading` through it (`:3478`). So shipping the file closes the last 読解 hole with no new server plumbing and no new item type. **"Closed" means no 読解 block is named in `unfilledBlocks`. It does not mean every 読解 大問 trains its real ねらい.** That second claim is the quality gap this section names, and its readiness preconditions are the list TASK-146 parked (a reject oracle distinct from `validateQuestionItem`, a bounded attempt budget, a disagreement-rate harness, a human certification step, one wired command). TASK-173.9.14 is exactly the condition TASK-146's note reserves for reopening it, "a future goal asks the model for item content the corpus cannot source", so the new task records the dependency on TASK-146 rather than quietly changing its parked status.

## 11. D-8. Who certifies an authored item when nobody in the project can read the language

§10's plan said "a human reviews every row before anything ships". **That premise is false and was wrong when it was written: the owner of this app does not read Japanese, and neither does any contributor.** The 読解 work exists because the owner is learning the language, not checking it. So no route to this block has a reader in it, and the certification has to be built out of what the project actually has.

**Why the gap is asymmetric between the two constructions.** A masked-slot row needs no reader: the passage prints the answer word, the collocation frame is uniquely filled corpus-wide, and restore-to-source is byte equality, so a correct key is a provable property of the file. A comprehension row's key is a judgment about meaning ("which option states the author's view"), and with no reader in the loop, the only thing standing between a wrong key and a shipped store is the model that wrote the prose being graded by models of the same family. That is the whole risk of the §10 route, named here rather than discovered later.

**Decision, and it is a product decision, not an oversight.** The owner was offered the choice with this tradeoff stated (cloze key = provable but trains vocabulary-in-context under a comprehension ねらい, versus authored comprehension = the real exam skill with keys no reader can independently verify) and chose **authored comprehension**. The N3 大問6 store therefore ships comprehension items, and the certification is two-legged:

1. **Mechanical, CI, model-free:** the row is `construction: "authored"`, carries no `sourceIds` (it claims no external licence), its passage is in the block's N3 band, no `BLANK` appears in it, the four options are distinct, the store's stems span distinct ねらい, and the key positions are spread. These are the properties that do not require understanding meaning.
2. **Blind judge, with the evidence recorded in the row.** Each authored row carries `evidence`: the passage sentence that decides it. The judge answers from the passage alone with the key withheld and must agree with the recorded evidence, using the same freeze-time procedure and the same frozen-verdict files as the 中文 and 情報検索 stores (`prompts/reading-validator.md`, `{level}-choubun-rejected.json`). CI only ever reads that record; it never runs a model.

**A third leg was built, and it was wrong, so it is gone.** This section previously required the owner to review each row's argument from an English translation sheet, on the reasoning that "what a non-reader of Japanese can decide is whether a passage entails its answer". It cannot be decided that way. The translation is produced by the same model that wrote the passage and picked the key, so a sheet in English asks the owner to agree with the author's own reading of its own text, restated in a language the author happens to translate well. A mistranslation that flatters the key makes an unsolvable item look airtight, and the reviewer has no way to see it. That sheet existed at `.cache/judge/choubun-n3-owner-review.md`, was handed to the owner twice, and is deleted: a check that cannot fail is not a check, and asking a human to sign a formality buys false assurance rather than none. The honest count is two legs, both run by models.

**Residual risk, kept visible.** Two judge procedures are one model family, LESS independent than the two families behind §13's stores, and it is the family that authored the text, so agreement is correlated error, not confirmation. Nothing in this design catches a key that is wrong in a way the authoring model also believes. What does bound it: the `evidence` field makes any later reader's disagreement checkable against a specific sentence instead of a whole passage, the mechanical screens catch the shape errors that do not depend on meaning, and the real oracle is a learner using the app and finding an item that does not resolve. That is a weaker guarantee than the cloze route offered, and §11 exists so the weakness is on the record rather than implied.

## 12. D-9. 読解 serves no fallback row, and what that route costs at program scale

Two decisions, both recorded while the authored screens moved into one module (`packages/japanese/scripts/reading-authored-corpus.ts`, TASK-173.9.17) so the four conversion tasks can call them without editing them.

**The serving rule: a level's 読解 大問 answers from that level's rows or comes up short.** Three legs make it true, and only the third was missing. (1) A level with no store file returns the empty array and each picker answers null before its walk, so the block reports its hole (TASK-173.1). (2) A composed row is refused in real mode unless its key was falsified offline (`isRefused`, `apps/server/src/services/exam-service.ts`), so an unjudged authored key never reaches a learner. (3) A row that sits in the RIGHT file but carries another level's stamp was still served, because the loader keys on the filename (`apps/server/src/services/exam-corpus.ts`, `loadReadingLevel`) and the assembled item honestly carries the row's own stamp, so item-engine's `validateAnchors` agreed with it. That hole was unreachable until now and becomes reachable with TASK-173.9.18 through TASK-173.9.21: those tasks drain a masked-slot store and refill the same file with authored rows, so a borrowed row left behind would print an N1 白書 window under N3's 大問6 heading and the paper would read as full. The rule is written once (`servableReadingRow`) and called by all six pickers, and **refusing means skip and continue**, not end the walk: one mis-stamped entry must not blindside the rest of an honest store. It changes no shipped behaviour because every `packages/japanese/data/reading/*.json` store self-labels the level of its own filename, the borrowed N4/N3 情報検索 rows included, which are re-stamped in-file precisely so their anchor matches the item.

**Why no fallback, when a fallback is easy.** The tempting substitution is exactly the one this doc has been arguing against in §2, §4 and §6: serve adjacent-level content so the 大問 stops showing up as a hole. It would make the receipt quiet and the ねらい wrong. A 長文 heading with a 中文-length passage under it, or an N1 window at N3, trains a skill the 大問 does not ask and reports to the learner that they have read it. The owner's standing instruction is that 読解 is finished at a level only when each 大問 trains its real ねらい with depth, so a filled-but-wrong block is not further along than a named hole. A shortfall is a fact about the store; a fallback is a false fact.

**The risk acceptance, scaled.** §11 recorded correlated error for 8 rows judged twice. The instruction the owner gave on 2026-10-09 ("no fallback, create authored reading by AI, create as much as you like") extends that same instrument to roughly 300 authored rows and 600 blind judgments across N3, N4, N2 and N1, all inside the model family that writes the prose. What that means, on the record:

- The judge passes are the binding cost, not the authoring. Every row needs a key-less packet put in front of two independent passes, and its verdict frozen in `{level}-…-rejected.json` for CI to read. A task that skips that step has shipped an uncertified store, whatever the mechanical gate says.
- A systematic error now propagates instead of sitting in one file. If this family consistently reads one inference pattern as entailed, every level's 内容理解 inherits it, and nothing in the design can see it, because the check that would catch it is a reader of Japanese and the project has none (§11).
- What bounds it is unchanged and deliberately not strengthened with a fake third leg: `evidence` pins each key to one printed sentence so a later reader can check a disagreement against a specific span rather than a whole passage, the mechanical screens catch the shape errors that do not depend on meaning, and the real oracle is a learner who finds an item that does not resolve.
- The visible mitigation is that every store keeps its own rejection file next to it. A row that failed a pass stays out of the store with its verdict recorded, so the program's assurance is auditable per level rather than asserted.
