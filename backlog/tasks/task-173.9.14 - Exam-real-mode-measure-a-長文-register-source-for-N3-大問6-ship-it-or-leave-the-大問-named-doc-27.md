## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
This is the other half of the 長文 finding in doc-27, and it is the opposite diagnosis from N1. At N3 the floor is right and the corpus is wrong. Scored with coverageOf, the official N3 中文 measures 87.7% and the official N3 長文 88.9%, both clearing COVERAGE_FLOOR.N3 = 0.8 (packages/japanese/scripts/reading-chuubun-corpus.ts:103), so real N3 exam prose is EASIER than what the floor demands and the floor stays exactly where it is. The 白書 cannot reach it at 長文 length: the same exhaustive window search in the 500-700 rune band yields 1747 windows, a 79.9% ceiling, none clearing 0.80 and a p90 of 73.9%, about 8 points harder than official N3 material, because the register is administrative (生物多様性国家戦略, イベント・アトリビューション, スーパーコンピュータ). N3 大問6 prints 4 小問 (packages/item-engine/src/paper.ts:555) under a block whose missing question kind was supplied by TASK-173.9.12 (`itemTypes: ["long-reading"]`, packages/item-engine/src/paper.ts:344-347), so the block is now fillable in principle and bound only by source supply. The licensed alternatives doc-27 swept are thin for this genre: 青空文庫 is freely redistributable for expired works but its 随筆・評論 shelf is 4113 works of which 1972 are 新字旧仮名/旧字旧仮名 and only about 408 are by authors who died in 1960 or later, and the fetch script's own header records that Aozora pre-war prose and jawiki both measured under the coverage bar (packages/japanese/scripts/fetch-hakusho.ts:13-16, the note that the earlier measurement was at 中文 length). So the task is a measurement with a ship-or-refuse verdict, not a build with a guaranteed store.

Measurement (`bun run probe:n3-choubun`, N3 rank 3, official band 500-700 runes, all pages cached so the numbers are reproducible with no network): jawiki 150/150 pages fetched, 102 prose pages, 1312 paragraphs, 83 windows, 4 clearing COVERAGE_FLOOR.N3 = 0.8, 1 clearing CHOUBUN_COVERAGE_FLOOR = 0.85, disjoint buildable rows 1 (from 1 page) at 0.85 and 3 (from 3 pages) at 0.8, best window 0008--4+0008--5 at 557 runes / 85.6%. soumu 情報通信白書 令和6年版 100/100 pages, 84 prose pages, 478 paragraphs, 61 windows, 3 clear 0.8, 0 clear 0.85, 0 rows at 0.85 and 3 (3 pages) at 0.8, best 83.5%. npa 警察白書 令和6年版 100/100 pages, 94 prose pages, 1528 paragraphs, 59 windows, 3 clear 0.8, 0 clear 0.85, 0 rows at 0.85 and 2 (2 pages) at 0.8, best 82.1%. Verdict: AC#4, three arms and 2830 paragraphs yield 1 buildable row at the bar the 長文 gate enforces, so nothing ships and no floor moves.

How it was measured, because the AC asked for the shipped method not a new one: the probe runs the 白書 corpus's OWN extractor (factored out of scripts/fetch-hakusho.ts into the shared packages/japanese/scripts/html-prose.ts: scored Shift-JIS/UTF-8/EUC-JP decode, `<p>` extraction, the 4-attempt throttled fetch), the OWN instrument (coverageOf over readCumulativeVocabIndex(dataDir, levelsUpTo("N3")) at LEVEL_RANKS.N3), the OWN adjacency screens (choubunWindowsFor, 2-5 consecutive prose paragraphs each ≥150 runes), and the OWN batch builder (buildChoubunBatch) with only its coverage floor overridden, so each bar reports the rows THAT bar would accept through one algorithm. To make that possible, PassageBand became a parameter of choubunWindowsFor (scripts/reading-choubun-corpus.ts) and floor an optional parameter of buildChoubunEntry/buildChoubunBatch (scripts/gen-reading-choubun.ts, default CHOUBUN_COVERAGE_FLOOR), which leaves N1 behaviour byte-identical.

Deviations, recorded rather than smoothed over. AC#1(b) asks for "at least two non-環境省 PDL1.0 agency HTML publications": ONE is verified (総務省, https://www.soumu.go.jp/menu_kyotsuu/policy/tyosaku.html, credit 「出典：総務省ホームページ（当該ページのURL）」), and the second arm (警察庁) is measured but UNVERIFIED, because no reuse statement appears on any of the 100 白書 pages it fetched, its index is a frameset linking only the text, and four candidate terms URLs answer 404 (/kijun/riyo/riyo.htm, /kijun/riyo/index.htm, /copyrights/index.html, /home.htm).
