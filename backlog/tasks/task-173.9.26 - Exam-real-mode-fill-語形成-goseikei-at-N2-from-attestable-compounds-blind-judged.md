## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
語形成 is the N2-only 語彙 大問: PAPER_BLOCK_SHAPES["goseikei"] declares itemTypes: [] (packages/item-engine/src/paper.ts:330) and it prints at N2 大問3 with 5 小問 (paper.ts:587); doc-26 §3.3 and the corrected scope note in TASK-173.9.1 pin that no other level asks it (N3 has NO 語形成). The official ねらい, measured from the 2018 sample PDF (https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sample2018/pdf/N2V.pdf, 問題３): the sentence prints a PARTIAL COMPOUND beside a （　） blank and four SINGLE-KANJI options, exactly one of which completes the compound the sentence wants (結婚（　）→ 観, （　）水準 → 高, 日本（　）→ 式, （　）使用 → 未; the blank sits first or last). The task draft assumed three parts in circles and four orderings as options; the samples falsified that, as they falsified the 表記 draft before it. The item is corpus-checkable: the key is an attested pure-kanji N2-band compound, and every distractor kanji must complete the printed fragment to NO attested word in ANY band (an attested completion is the two-answer defect; the 表記 printed-kana-not-uniquely-attested precedent). The sentence is authored per the owner-approved pattern-driven route (doc-27 §11, the 用法/文章の文法/表記 family); the corpus gives authoring seeds only (57 of the 74 clean candidates occur in data/sentences/sentences.jsonl, LICENSE §8), and the printed sentence is this repo's own prose. Sourcing honesty: distractors are single kanji the learner has met (occurring in an N5–N2 band word), never same-pos-pool/single-group-fallback (apps/server/src/services/exam-service.ts:1276), so a served 語形成 can never trip the TASK-173.1 real-mode refusal. Depth standard: 5 小問 x 4 papers = 20 distinct rows at N2; below the floor the block declines and names the hole (TASK-173.4), never invents.
