## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Content half of the 173.9.5 split: fill a REAL N4 短文 block (大問4 naiyou-rikai-tanbun) with composed items the honesty gate admits. Passages are SINGLE verbatim Tatoeba sentences (packages/japanese/data/sentences/sentences.jsonl, CC BY 2.0) whose words are all within N5+N4 vocab, so difficulty is measured; multi-sentence stitch-ups are rejected because Tatoeba sentences are not a connected discourse, so they do not make a comprehensible 短文. Question + 4 options + key authored offline by agy --print (packages/acp direct-cli, zero AI SDK); the answer key falsified by a BLIND independent judge (packages/japanese/prompts/reading-validator.md, spawned via packages/acp SessionController as OPENCODE with an explicit non-Gemini-family model; kilo rejected: kilo/kilo-auto/free is a router with no stable model identity). The judge never sees answerIndex and returns only its defensible-option list; the SCRIPT diffs judge pick vs author key (see 2026-10-03 decision notes). Author+judge disagree, or the judge finds a second defensible option, or the sentence cannot support a clean single answer -> DECLINE the item (refuse-not-fabricate). Ship a handful of clean items, not forty mushy ones. validated:true means screened, not proven; validatedBy records the judge agent + resolved model id. The frozen batch gets one human eyeball before it is trusted into real.

#4 TWO QUALITY GAPS, both now screens AND gate proofs:
(a) answer-not-named-by-translation. The answer must share an English content token with the source sentence's own translation, so the item measures reading rather than guessing. Measured effect: 423 -> 169 candidate slots on a 7,386-sentence sample, about 60% of otherwise-valid slots removed. Note 話 SURVIVES for "To change the subject" - and correctly so: JMdict sense 2 of 話 is "topic/subject", so the translation really does name it. My earlier use of 話 as the counter-example was wrong.
(b) distractor-collides-with-answer. A distractor may not be another English label for the answer (same DOMINANT JMdict sense). This one took two corrections, both found by measuring rather than reasoning: comparing ALL sense groups made 私/角 collide on the token "affair" (私 "personal (affairs, etc.)" vs 色 "paramour") and reported 22,990 colliding pairs out of 842 nouns - nonsense. Dropping the JMdict boilerplate (etc, esp) and comparing only sense group 0 gives 346 pairs, and the survivors are the real ones: 一日/七日, 一日/今日, 警察/警官. I also TRIED AND REJECTED a shared-kanji rule: it fires on 一日/一人, where the translation disambiguates and both are legitimate options. Measured honestly: 0 of these collisions occurred in the shipped batch, so this screen is insurance against hand-edited rows and future vocabulary growth, not a filter doing work today.
Also: one row per ANSWER WORD per batch, enforced by the generator and re-proved by the gate as duplicate-answer. The bigger pool had filled a batch with three 部屋 rows and two 風 rows, and a 大問4 printing them asks the same question three times in different clothes.
